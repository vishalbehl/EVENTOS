# Registration Portal FAQ

## How do I register?

Complete the registration form and submit the required information. Payment (if applicable) must be completed before registration is confirmed.

---

## I did not receive my confirmation email.

Please:

1. Check your Spam/Junk folder.
2. Verify that you entered the correct email address.
3. Wait a few minutes for email delivery.

If you still have not received it, contact the event support team.

---

## Can I update my registration details?

Yes, depending on Organizer settings. Some information may be editable before the registration deadline.

---

## Can I transfer my registration to someone else?

Unless specifically permitted by the Organizer, registrations are non-transferable.

---

## How do I access the Speaker Portal?

Please use your **registered email address** or the **access code provided by the Organizer via email**.

---

## What if I forget my access code?

Use the registered email recovery option or contact the Organizer for assistance.

---

## Can I cancel my registration?

Cancellation and refund policies vary by event. Please refer to the event-specific cancellation policy.

---

## Who should I contact for technical support?

Please contact the event support desk using the contact information provided in your confirmation email.
